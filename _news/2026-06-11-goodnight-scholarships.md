Two of my outstanding students at Wake Technical Community College have received the highly competitive and prestigious Goodnight Scholarship from NC State University. The Goodnight Scholarship is a full-tuition scholarship awarded to undergraduates pursuing STEM and education degrees.

Karolaine is from Sau Paulo, Brazil and came to the United States a few years ago as an au pair. Last year Karolaine participated in the STARS research experience for WTCC students hosted at NC State, where she was able to gain experience in engineering research. Josh was born in Nairobi, Kenya and has resided with his family in Cary, NC for most of his life. Karolaine and Josh are both studying electrical engineering at NC State. 

I am extremely proud of Josh and Karolaine. I cannot imagine two more deserving students. Congratulations to both of you! -->
